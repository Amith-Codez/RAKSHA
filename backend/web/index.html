The browser extension

Three clicks between your parents and a scam.

The danger is the click. RAKSHA sits in the toolbar, reads only the ad you point at, and answers in the side panel before anyone opens a link or pays.

  1. Add it to ChromeDownload the extension and unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped raksha-extension folder. A short tutorial opens by itself.
  2. Pin the shieldClick the puzzle piece in Chrome’s toolbar and pin RAKSHA, so the orange shield is always one click away.
  3. Click any adOn Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp Web or any site: click the shield (or press Alt Shift R), then click the ad. That’s it.

The investigation

Agents are on it…

Eleven agents. One argument. A straight answer.

No single AI decides. Specialists gather facts, a Prosecutor and a Defender fight over every charge, and an Arbiter settles only what they can't agree on.

1

Read

The Extractor pulls out every checkable claim: promised returns, names, links, phone numbers, SEBI numbers.

2

Investigate

The Router sends the right specialists: SEBI and RBI registries, website, phone and UPI checks, the Link agent (where the button really goes), community memory and known scam patterns.

3

Debate

The Prosecutor files charges. The Defender accepts, disputes, or asks a specialist to check a fact.

4

Verdict

The Arbiter rules on anything still contested, then explains the answer in plain English and Hindi.
